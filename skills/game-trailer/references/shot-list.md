# Shot list template

| # | Length | Camera | Action | Caption | Sound cue |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4 s | slow push-in | the hook: your enemy / world / big moment | 2-4 words | low hit, ambience starts |
| 2 | 5 s | locked or slow pan | the core verb (hide, jump, build, shoot) | what the player does | a voice line or action sound |
| 3 | 5 s | tracking | the pressure (enemy near, timer, chase) | the rule of the game | heartbeat / music builds |
| 4 | 3.5 s | handheld feel | escalation: something goes wrong | the stakes | stinger on the cut |
| 5 | 3 s | extreme close-up | the scariest / coolest frame | none | one strong line, then silence |
| T | 4.7 s | title card | game name + one-line genre | - | impact |

Rules:
- Show the hook in the first 3 seconds; many viewers leave after that.
- Real gameplay only; no fake features.
- Captions: all caps, 2-5 words, one idea each, same position every time.
- Cut on sound: hits and voice lines land on cuts or right after.
- End on the title; hold it at least 3 seconds.
